Looking for the word scramble game?

Step 1: teams and words. Step 2: place every word yourself. Step 3: set the time and create the game.

Each word can belong to only one team. If the same word is typed for two teams, it stays with the first team.
One word per line, then a comma and its clue. Example: ELEPHANT, Largest land animal
Teams play one at a time. The team that finishes fastest wins. If nobody finishes, the team with the most correct words wins.
